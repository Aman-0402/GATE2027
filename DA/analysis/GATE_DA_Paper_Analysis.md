## What it means
- Probability & Statistics, Programming/DSA and Machine Learning together carry about 47 of 100 marks on average; plan the most time for them.
- Databases & Warehousing swings a lot (18 marks in 2026, 7 in 2024); the questions seen cover SQL/relational algebra, functional dependencies, B+ trees, ER models and data cubes.
- AI is about 8–9 marks: search (uninformed, A*, minimax/alpha-beta), logic, and Bayesian networks appear in these papers.
- Linear algebra questions seen use eigenvalues, subspaces, singular values and projection-type matrices; calculus seen covers continuity/differentiability, maxima/minima, limits and series.
- Marking: 10 GA (15 marks) are the cheapest marks; MSQ/NAT carry no negative marking, so attempt every one.
