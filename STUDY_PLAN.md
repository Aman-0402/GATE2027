# GATE CS 2027 — 14-Week Study Plan

**Assumptions:** start Monday 2 Nov 2026; first reported exam day is 6 Feb 2027 (confirm your paper date on the official GATE site). About 3 hours on weekdays and 6 hours on weekends (~27 hrs/week). If you have more time, add it to Phase 1 and the mocks. The weights come from `GATE_CS_Important_Topics.md`.

## Daily routine (every day)
- **30 min General Aptitude** (15 marks per paper): quant one day, verbal the next, alternating.
- **Study block:** the week's subject (see below). Read the notes PDF, then solve the past questions for that topic from the subject PDF.
- **15 min error log:** write every question you got wrong, why, and the fix. Revisit the log every Sunday.

## Phase 1: Learn and practise by subject (Weeks 1–9)
| Week | Starts | Subject (marks/paper) | Focus (highest-weight topics first) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 Nov | Engineering Maths A (~15 total) | Probability, linear algebra |
| 2 | 9 Nov | Engineering Maths B | Sets/groups/lattices, graph theory, combinatorics, logic, calculus |

| 6 | 7 Dec | Computer Networks (8.1) | IP/subnetting, TCP, data link, routing |
| 7 | 14 Dec | Theory of Computation (7.7) | DFA/minimisation, CFL/PDA, decidability |
| 8 | 21 Dec | Computer Organization (7.5) + Digital Logic (6.9) | Cache, pipeline, addressing; K-maps, number systems, counters |
| 9 | 28 Dec | Databases (7.0) + Compiler Design (5.9) | Normalization, SQL, transactions, B+ trees; parsing, data-flow |

Each week: Mon–Thu learn from the notes and solve the Tier 1 topics, Fri finish the rest, Sat solve the week's past questions and the practice questions, Sun a 1-hour test on the week's subject (15–20 questions picked from the subject PDF) plus the error-log review.

## Rules of thumb
- **Attempt all NAT questions** (no negative marking). On MCQs, a wrong 1-mark answer costs ⅓ mark and a wrong 2-mark answer costs ⅔ mark.
- **Pick questions in order of confidence:** do General Aptitude and the short 1-mark questions first, then the 2-mark ones.
- **Do not start a new topic in the last 10 days.**
- If you fall behind, drop Tier 3 topics rather than skipping the mocks.
- Redo the practice questions you got wrong at 1 day, 1 week and 3 weeks.
